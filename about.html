The Journey

From One Table to 200+ Homes

  1. 2014

    A small studio in Malviya Nagar

    Two people, one drafting table and a simple promise: design homes around how families actually live.

  2. 2016

    Our first turnkey home

    For the first time we handled design, civil work and furniture under one roof — and never looked back.

  3. 2018

    Our own craftsmen

    We built an in-house team of carpenters and site supervisors so the quality stays in our hands.

  4. 2020

    A 3D design studio

    Photo-real 3D views arrived, so every client could walk through their home before work began.

  5. 2023

    150+ happy families

    Homes across Delhi NCR — from compact 1 BHKs to complete renovations with pooja units and feature walls.

  6. Today

    200+ projects and counting

    The same promise as day one: thoughtful design, honest pricing and an on-time handover.

The Founders

The People Behind Interior Core

Arjun Mehta

Arjun trained as an interior architect and spent his early years designing hotels and restaurants before starting Interior Core in 2014.

He leads every concept — layouts, materials and lighting — and believes a home should be built around daily routines, not passing trends.
