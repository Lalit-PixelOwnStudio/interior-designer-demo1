FAQ

Questions, Answered

How long does a full home interior take?

It depends on the size and scope. We share a clear timeline with you at the design stage, before any work starts, and keep you updated throughout.

Do you handle civil, electrical and plumbing work?

Yes. Our turnkey projects cover civil changes, wiring, plumbing, false ceilings, furniture and finishing — all managed by our own team.

Will I see the design before work begins?

Yes. You get 2D layouts and 3D views of your space, and we refine them with you until you are happy.

Can you work on just one room?

Absolutely. We take on single rooms — a bedroom, living room, kitchen or pooja unit — as well as complete homes.

Which areas do you work in?

We work across Delhi NCR, with projects in Malviya Nagar, Vasant Kunj, Naraina, Faridabad and more.

How do I get a quote?

Send us your details using the form below or on WhatsApp. We'll set up a consultation and site visit, then share a detailed quote.
