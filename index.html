How We Work

Five Steps to Your New Space

Scroll or tap a step to see what happens. One team and one point of contact from start to finish.

  1. 01 Step 01 / 05

    Consultation

    We sit down with you to understand how you live, what you love and what you want to spend.

    • Your lifestyle, family and storage needs
    • Budget and scope, room by room
    • Style references and mood boards
    You getA clear brief we both agree on
  2. 02 Step 02 / 05

    Site Visit & Measurement

    Our team visits the site and measures every wall, window and service point before any design starts.

    • Exact measurements of every room
    • Electrical, plumbing and structure checked
    • Photos and notes of existing conditions
    You getAn accurate site plan
  3. 03 Step 03 / 05

    2D & 3D Design

    We plan the layout, then show you photo-real 3D views, and refine them with you until it feels right.

    • Space-planned 2D layouts

  4. 04 Step 04 / 05

    Execution

    Our own carpenters and site team build it, with regular updates so you always know where things stand.

    • In-house craftsmen and site supervisor

    • Quality checks at every stage

  5. 05 Step 05 / 05

    Handover

    A final walkthrough together, a deep clean and styling, so you can move straight in.

    • Walkthrough and snag check with you

FAQ

Questions, Answered

How long does a full home interior take?

It depends on the size and scope. We share a clear timeline with you at the design stage, before any work starts, and keep you updated throughout.

Do you handle civil, electrical and plumbing work?

Yes. Our turnkey projects cover civil changes, wiring, plumbing, false ceilings, furniture and finishing, all managed by our own team.

Will I see the design before work begins?

Yes. You get 2D layouts and 3D views of your space, and we refine them with you until you are happy.

Can you work on just one room?

Absolutely. We take on single rooms (a bedroom, living room, kitchen or pooja unit) as well as complete homes.

Which areas do you work in?

We work across Delhi NCR, with projects in Malviya Nagar, Vasant Kunj, Naraina, Faridabad and more.

How do I get a quote?

Send us your details using the form below or on WhatsApp. We'll set up a consultation and site visit, then share a detailed quote.
